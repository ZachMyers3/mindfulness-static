## What is on offer

Three things, taught with care. Each one is meant to be a way of returning — to the
body, to the breath, to the present moment. None of them require experience. None of
them require flexibility. The only preparation is a willingness to notice.

## Slow yoga

The yoga we teach is unhurried. Postures are held for several breaths and built
gradually. There is no music, no mirror, no countdown, no heated room. The pace is set
by the breath, and the breath is given room to settle at its own speed.

Classes are suitable for beginners and long-time practitioners alike. Every posture has
options — chair, wall, block, blanket — and your teacher will offer alternatives for any
body, any injury, any stage of life. Yoga is not a performance here. It is a practice.

## Breathwork

The breathwork sessions are shorter than a yoga class and ask less of the body. They
are a quiet ten to {{TODO_ZACH: duration, e.g. thirty}} minutes of guided breathing —
sometimes seated, sometimes lying down — designed to settle the nervous system and
lengthen the exhale.

You do not need any experience. You do not need to sit a particular way. You only need
to be willing to breathe a little more slowly than usual and notice what changes.

## Quiet gathering

Once a month we open the room for an unhurried afternoon of practice — a longer
breathwork session, some gentle movement, and a long rest at the end with tea. There is
no agenda. There is nothing to take notes on. People stay or leave as they need to.

## What we do not teach

A short list, so there is no confusion:

- We do not teach hot yoga or heated classes.
- We do not teach power yoga or flow-as-exercise.
- We do not teach handstands, arm balances, or any posture that asks the body for more
  than the breath can give.
- We do not teach yoga as a path to a particular body shape, weight, or aesthetic.
- We do not use music as a workout beat.

If you are looking for any of the above, this is not the studio. There are many good
studios in {{TODO_ZACH: city}} that do that work; we are happy to recommend one.

## A note on accessibility

Every class is taught with full accessibility in mind. The room is step-free, the
bathroom is on the same floor, and every posture has a chair or wall version. If you
have a question about whether a class is right for you, [send a note](/contact/) and we
will be glad to talk it through.
