## What we collect

Nothing, by default. We do not use analytics tools, advertising trackers, or any
third-party scripts that profile visitors. We do not set cookies. We do not record
your IP address beyond what your browser shares with the web server to deliver this
page, which is not stored.

## What we do collect

If you choose to email us, we receive the contents of your message and the email
address you sent it from. We keep those messages so we can reply and so we don't ask
the same question twice. We do not add you to a marketing list and we do not share
your address with anyone.

If you attend a class, we may keep a small paper note with your name and emergency
contact so the room knows who is present in case of a medical event. That note is
shredded after the class series ends, unless you ask us to keep it longer.
