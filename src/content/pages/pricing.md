## How we price

We try to keep the room open. Pricing is set on a sliding scale so that the practice is
within reach for steady students on a range of incomes. Please choose the rate that fits
your situation honestly — there is no application, no proof, and no awkward conversation.

No one is turned away for lack of funds. If the rates below are still not workable,
[send a note](/contact/) and we will work something out.

> The first class is always free. New students do not need to decide about a package
> before they arrive.
