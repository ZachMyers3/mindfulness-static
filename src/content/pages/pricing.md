> $10  - drop in (this is good for one regularly scheduled class)

> $80 - one month unlimited (unlimited regularly scheduled classes for 30 days, starts the day of purchase)

> $40 - private lessons (1-2 students, request this through our app) - [https://app.mindfulnessandmovement.org/request](https://app.mindfulnessandmovement.org/request)

For private group classes (3+ students) please email the studio or send a request through our app and we will get back to you as soon as possible!

## How to pay

If you would like to pay with cash or Venmo, to avoid tax and fees, please book your class unpaid through our app and pay in studio before your class starts.

You can book and pay with a credit card through the app - tax and fees are included on the app. You will book your class and then pay for your purchase option separately. The purchase option that you choose will auto apply to your booking!

Link to our mobile app: [https://app.mindfulnessandmovement.org/about](https://app.mindfulnessandmovement.org/about)
